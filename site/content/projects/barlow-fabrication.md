What started as fixing my parents' broken electric recliner has grown into its own business. I design and sell 3D-printed replacement parts for broken electric reclining couches and chairs, keeping these assemblies out of the landfill and saving people money on costly replacements.

Many recliner models fail because of a single plastic "moving block" that isn't sold separately, so the manufacturer's answer is a full motor-and-actuator assembly. A printed block and an afternoon with a screwdriver fix it for a fraction of the cost.

The project now has a dedicated home. For the full list of supported models, purchasing options and free repair guides, visit [BarlowFabrication.com](https://barlowfabrication.com) or reach out at [contact@barlowfabrication.com](mailto:contact@barlowfabrication.com).

Behind the storefront is a Go and Angular platform with authentication, a customer portal, order and inventory management, and S3-compatible storage for product imagery, all running on the same Docker Swarm as my other projects.
