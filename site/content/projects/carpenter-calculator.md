A simple calculator app for the kind of arithmetic that comes up in a workshop: fractions of an inch, board feet and the like. Every calculation happens on the device. There is no account, no analytics and no network access.

The app's [privacy policy](/carpenter-calculator/privacy-policy/) is published here because the store listing requires one, and it fits in a sentence: nothing is collected.
