## Key projects

### Pillar 2A credit concentration risk validation

*2026*  ·  a European challenger bank

I coordinated an independent validation of a Pillar 2A credit concentration risk capital model for a European challenger bank, leading a small team to a supervisory submission deadline. The model applied a Basel IRB baseline with add-ons for single-name concentration through a granularity adjustment, for sector concentration through a multi-factor model carrying intra-sector and inter-sector correlations, and for geographic diversification through inter-portfolio correlations. The validation covered statistical segmentation testing, a parameter inventory across the model's vintages, an independent Python replication of the production model, and a simpler HHI challenger benchmark.

**Skills**: Pillar 2 \| ICAAP \| Basel IRB \| Concentration Risk \| Model Validation \| Python \| Kedro
