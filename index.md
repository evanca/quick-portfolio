## Key projects

### Pillar 2A credit concentration risk validation

*2026*  ·  a European challenger bank

Coordinated delivery of an independent validation of a Pillar 2A credit concentration risk capital model, managing two consultants across eight work streams from kick-off to signed-off report in eight weeks. The validation tested a Basel IRB baseline carrying three add-ons, a granularity adjustment for single-name concentration, a multi-factor sector model with intra-sector and inter-sector correlations, and inter-portfolio correlations for geographic diversification. Work covered statistical segmentation testing, a parameter inventory across five model vintages, an independent Python replication of the production model, and a simpler HHI challenger benchmark. 38 observations were raised, and 87 per cent were accepted or partially accepted ahead of the final report.

**Skills**: Pillar 2 \| ICAAP \| Basel IRB \| Concentration Risk \| Model Validation \| Python \| Kedro
